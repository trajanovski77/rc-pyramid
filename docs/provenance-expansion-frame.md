# Provenance-sample expansion: sampling frame and procedure

**Status:** Specified 2026-08-20, not executed. Referenced from manuscript Section 9
(Limitations) as the systematic expansion that precedes the full audit.
**Origin:** Internal review of 2026-08-20: "why only 8 statements?" The current provenance
set is purposive (every quantity the project itself used), which supports existence claims
but no rate estimate. This document fixes a defensible sampling frame **before** any new
statement is verified, so the expansion cannot be steered by its results.

## Sampling frame, fixed now

The frame is **every external quantitative benchmark or theoretical claim asserted with a
numeric value in the eleven reviews of manuscript Table 2**, identified by reading each
review once and listing every sentence that (a) states a number attributed to another work
and (b) concerns benchmark performance, capacity, dispersion, a threshold, or an efficiency
figure. Duplicated claims across reviews are recorded once per review, because transmission
across reviews is part of what is being measured.

Rationale for this frame rather than a corpus sample: the eleven reviews are the field's
own synthesis layer, the place where a lost qualifier propagates furthest, and the frame is
enumerable and auditable without the (still pending) Stage-1 screen. A random sample of the
screened corpus remains the full audit's job (analysis A3/A4 territory) and is not
duplicated here.

## Procedure, identical to the original pass

Each listed statement is verified under manuscript Section 6.3's procedure: cited source
first, then backward and forward citation chase and exact-phrase search; verified only when
value, metric, unit, conditions, and attribution all agree; otherwise corrected, downgraded,
or removed, with the disposition and the primary quotation recorded per statement in this
repository. Every verification is logged with date and source location. Automated retrieval
is permitted for locating and quoting records, but every disposition requires a human
reading of the quoted primary text before it is entered.

## Pre-committed reporting rules

1. The denominator is the full enumerated list; statements that cannot be resolved are
   reported as unresolved, never dropped.
2. The result is reported as a rate **for this frame** (quantitative claims in RC reviews,
   2009-2026), not for the literature at large.
3. The original eight-statement set is reported separately and never pooled with the frame,
   because its selection mechanism differs.
4. If the frame yields fewer than 15 statements, the expansion is reported as descriptive
   only and no rate is quoted.

## Effort estimate

The original pass averaged roughly one hour per statement including the citation chase.
The eleven reviews are expected to yield on the order of 20-40 qualifying statements, so
this is two to five working days for one investigator, with a second reader for the
dispositions strongly recommended after the single-investigator failure recorded in
deviation D15.
